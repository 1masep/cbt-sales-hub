# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner of CBT Hospitality Supplies (cbthospitalitysupplies.com), a wholesale supplier of bath, bedding, kitchen and restaurant goods to hotels, rentals and restaurants. Not technical. They sit down at a laptop or desktop at the start or end of the day to see what sold, check anything flagged or drafted by the AI, fix mistakes, and download the sheet to Excel for their books.

## Product Purpose

Every sale, whether from the WooCommerce website, point of sale, or a document the AI read, lands in one trustworthy sheet without retyping. Success is the owner trusting the numbers enough to stop keeping a separate spreadsheet: totals match to the cent, nothing is missing or doubled, and anything uncertain is clearly waiting for them rather than silently wrong.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, trustworthy. It should feel like the company's own back-office tool: quietly confident like a good accounting product (Stripe, QuickBooks Online), using CBT's own colors and logo rather than a generic SaaS look. Plain-spoken language; no jargon, no hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS dashboards: purple gradients, glass cards, giant hero metrics with sparkline confetti.
- Busy "growth" analytics tools that shout with color and motion.
- Dense developer tools that assume keyboard shortcuts and technical vocabulary.
- Anything that makes a draft or warning look like an error the owner caused.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first, and exact.** Money and counts are the content; give them the clearest type, alignment and contrast. Never round or hide cents.
2. **Calm until something needs you.** Neutral by default; color and emphasis are reserved for what needs a decision (drafts, review flags, connection problems).
3. **Say it plainly.** Every label and message is written for a non-technical owner and says what to do next.
4. **Their company, their tool.** CBT's colors and logo make it feel owned, without turning it into a marketing page.
5. **Trust through provenance.** Always show where a sale came from (website, point of sale, AI) and what was checked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum, aiming higher for text: the owner wants things easy to read, so favor larger body text (16px+), strong contrast for numbers and labels, and generous click targets. Never rely on color alone for meaning (pair with icons or words). Respect reduced-motion preferences.
